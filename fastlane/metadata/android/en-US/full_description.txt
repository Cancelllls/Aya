Aya is a comprehensive, offline-first Islamic companion app built with Flutter. It provides everything a Muslim needs for daily worship in one elegant, ad-free interface.

<b>Prayer Times & Adhan</b>
• 14 calculation methods with offline fallback — no internet required
• Native adhan playback with 8 reciters (Mishary, Abdul Basit, Madinah, and more)
• Per-prayer adhan toggle with fajr-specific reciter
• Pre-adhan reminders (5/10/15/20 min before) with vibrate/voice/both modes
• Flip-to-silence and volume-key stop gestures

<b>Quran Reader</b>
• 10 Qira'at supported — Hafs, Warsh, Qaloon, Shu'bah, Duri, Susi, Bazzi, Qunbul, Hisham, Ibn Dhakwan
• 12 reciters with per-ayah timestamp sync — bundled offline
• 6 tafsir editions — Al-Muyassar, Al-Jalalayn, Al-Qurtubi, Ibn Abbas, Al-Waseet, Al-Baghawi
• Continuous mushaf mode or ayah-by-ayah reading
• Auto-bookmarking and full-text Arabic/English verse search

<b>Hadith</b>
• 13 collections — Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasai, Ibn Majah, Malik, and more
• Arabic & English with grading badges (Sahih, Hasan, Da'if)
• FTS5 full-text search across all books
• Hadith explanation (Sharh) and grading (Takhreej)

<b>Azkar & Supplications</b>
• 11 categories — Morning, Evening, Post-Prayer, Daily Duas, 99 Names of Allah, and more
• 60+ authentic adhkar with Arabic, transliteration, translation, and references
• Digital Tasbih counter with custom presets

<b>Additional Features</b>
• Prayer Tracker with daily/monthly/yearly views and statistics
• Qibla Compass with Kaaba alignment detection
• 8 home screen widgets — Prayer Times, Verse of the Day, Dhikr, Hadith, Tasbih, Hijri Date, Next Prayer, Asma ul Husna
• Audio download manager for offline Quran listening
• 6 theme presets — Dark, Light, Sepia, OLED Black, Adaptive Dark and Light
• Full Arabic and English interface with RTL/LTR support
• 100% offline-first — Quran, Hadith, Azkar, Prayer Times, and Adhan all work without internet

<b>Open Source</b>
Aya is free, open-source software with no ads. Licensed under MIT.
